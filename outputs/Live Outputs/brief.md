# Output Tray launch brief

Codex made the file. Why am I still looking for it?

A compact native tray brings real files from one explicitly watched folder within reach.

- Preview the output.
- Open it in your preferred app.
- Drag the actual file into your next tool.
- Reveal it in Finder.

Prepared by Codex during the October 9 rehearsal. Project and task labels are registered explicitly.
